About the area

What the City says Doheny Village is

The City of Dana Point says its Doheny Village plan aims to preserve and enhance the eclectic combination of commercial, light industrial and residential mixed uses in the area. Its FAQ describes light industrial uses on the west side, residential on upper floors along Doheny Park Road, and commercial or residential uses on the east side.

The same FAQ names a variety of housing types, including multi-family housing, mobile homes and live-work units. The City Council adopted a zoning code update for the village on July 20, 2021, and the California Coastal Commission approved the plan on February 9, 2023.

The city lists connecting the village to the beach as a priority, especially at Doheny Park Road and Pacific Coast Highway. We use the City's description and do not guess at exact boundaries.

Commercial angle

Why plumbing for a mixed district looks different

A single block here can hold a machine shop, a cafe and apartments above a storefront. Each has different plumbing needs, and the problems are different from a home on a quiet street.

Restaurants and cafes send grease and food waste to the drain. When a line backs up repeatedly, hydro jetting cleans the pipe wall and a scheduled cleaning keeps it from returning. Shops and light industrial units often have floor drains and sinks that carry solids, so drain cleaning and a camera look matter.

A multi-tenant building adds another layer. Shared lines mean one tenant's blockage can back up into another unit, so knowing where each tenant's line joins the main saves time. See commercial plumbing for how those calls are handled.

Local note

Permits, tenants and who to call

Many commercial plumbing jobs need a permit. The City of Dana Point's Building and Safety Division, at (949) 248-3564, can say whether yours does. Plumbing work follows the California Plumbing Code, which is Part 5 of Title 24 and is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code. According to the City of Dana Point, permits are generally required for almost all construction. In a rented space, the lease usually says who is responsible for repairs, so check it and tell the landlord when a line fails.

Capistrano Beach is the coastal neighborhood nearby, and it has its own page: see Capistrano Beach plumbing. Your water bill shows which utility serves your address, and a leak on the street side of the meter is generally the utility's concern.
