About the area

What Monarch Beach is, and where it sits

Wikipedia calls Monarch Beach a small neighborhood located within the city of Dana Point, taking its name from Monarch Bay. It lists the Monarch Beach Resort and Links, Salt Creek Beach and a picnic area among the nearby places.

The Dana Point Times reports that a judge ruled Dana Point would keep the Monarch Beach coastal strip as the city was being formed. Dana Point became a city on January 1, 1989.

Local real estate guides describe gated enclaves such as Monarch Bay and Monarch Bay Terrace and a mix of single-family homes and condominiums. We use those as background only, because gate rules and boundaries belong to each community association.

Larger homes

What larger homes ask of their plumbing

This is general plumbing information, not a claim about any one Monarch Beach home. A bigger house usually has more bathrooms, more fixtures and longer runs between the water heater and the farthest tap. That raises the stakes on a single failed part.

A long run can mean a wait for hot water, and a tankless water heater or a recirculation setup is a common conversation. Hard water is another reason to look at a water softener or filtration system, since scale builds up in heaters and fixtures over time.

If a home has more than one water heater, a failure of one can go unnoticed until the second is stressed. Ask for each heater's age and condition during any visit.

Safety

If a water heater leaks or fails before we arrive

Water heaters combine water, gas or power and heat, so a few steps matter.

Shut off the cold supply

Use the valve above the heater.

Turn off gas or power

Use the heater's switch or the breaker.

Smell gas? Leave first

Leave the home and call SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 or 911 from outside.

Keep clear of hot water

A leaking tank can be scalding.

Local note

Gated communities, permits and the neighbors down the coast

Several streets here sit behind gates. Tell us the gate or guard instructions when you call, and check with your community association before exterior work. We will confirm the address and the access plan before anyone is scheduled.

The City of Dana Point's Building and Safety Division, at (949) 248-3564, can say whether a project needs a permit. Plumbing work follows the California Plumbing Code, which is Part 5 of Title 24 and is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code. According to the City of Dana Point, permits are generally required for almost all construction. Niguel Shores sits just up the coast and has its own page: see Niguel Shores plumbing.

Your water bill shows which utility serves your address. If water is running in the street, call the district. If it is running inside your property line, call us.
