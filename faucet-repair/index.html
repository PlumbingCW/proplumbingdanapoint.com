Emergency Faucet Repair in Dana Point, CA

Most faucet problems are slow drips. A few are not: a handle that will not shut off, a supply line that has split under the sink or a shower valve spraying behind a wall. Emergency faucet repair in Dana Point covers those, and the first move is the shutoff valve beneath the fixture.

Faucet and valve failures that cannot wait

Some faucet problems do damage fast. The dispatcher will ask which of these you have.

  • ✓
    A handle that will not turn off. A stripped stem or a failed cartridge leaves water running. Close the shutoff under the sink or the main.
  • ✓
    A supply line spraying under a sink. Braided connectors split at the crimp. Water can fill a cabinet in minutes.
  • ✓
    A shower valve that leaks behind the wall. Stains on an adjoining ceiling or wall are the clue. The valve body or a joint behind it has failed.
  • ✓
    A tub faucet that pours water from the spout when the shower is on. A failed diverter. It is not urgent but it wastes water and tends to get worse.
  • ✓
    An outdoor spigot that floods when opened. A failed packing or a cracked body behind the wall.
Why it drips

What is inside a dripping faucet

Faucets differ in design but not in logic. A compression faucet has a rubber washer that wears. A cartridge faucet has a sealed cartridge that fails. A ceramic disc faucet has discs that scratch or crack. The repair is to replace the worn part or the whole faucet.

A single drip per second adds up to a surprising amount of water over a month, and the cost shows on the bill. A faucet repair is one of the more economical plumbing jobs and often pays for itself.

Where else

Shower valves, tubs and drains

Faucet repair extends to the other fixtures that control water in a bathroom. Each has its own habits.

Shower valve and showerhead

A showerhead that drips after use points to a worn valve cartridge. Mineral buildup in a head is a separate, simpler problem.

Tub faucet

Tub spouts leak at the handle or the spout. A diverter that sticks sends water to the wrong outlet.

Shower and tub drain clogs

Hair and soap collect in the trap. A cable through the drain usually clears it. See drain cleaning if it keeps recurring.

Supply valves and connectors

The small valves under sinks and behind toilets wear out. Replace them before they fail.

Local plumbing

Salt air, mineral scale and fixtures in Dana Point

Fixtures on the coast take more punishment. Salt air corrodes exterior spigots, hose bibs and exposed shutoffs faster than the same parts inland. A green or white crust at the base of an outdoor spigot is a sign of corrosion that will eventually fail.

Inside the house, mineral scale clogs aerators and shortens the life of cartridges. If your water leaves white scale on fixtures, see water softener and filtration for how to test your tap and what to ask the utility. In older homes around Lantern Village, the shutoff valves are often the original ones, and they should be replaced during any faucet repair.

Decision

Repair the faucet or replace it?

A faucet is worth repairing when the body is sound and the part is available.

Repair when

  • The faucet is in good condition and a cartridge or washer will fix it
  • The finish is intact
  • The model is common
  • You like the fixture

Replace when

  • The body is corroded or cracked
  • Parts are discontinued
  • The finish is peeling
  • You want a different style or feature

If a new faucet is going in alongside a sink or counter change, the plumber can also install a garbage disposal. See garbage disposal repair and installation for what that involves.

Codes and sources

What code and which office apply

Faucet work is minor, but supply connections and shutoff valves still follow the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). Water-saving faucet flow limits come from state rules, so a replacement faucet should be labeled for California use.
