What this site collects

Dana Point Pro Plumbing does not use forms on this website. We do not ask you to type personal details into a page. The information we receive comes mainly from phone calls, and from basic technical logs that web hosting normally keeps, such as IP address and browser type.

Phone calls

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

When you call, we may take your name, phone number, address and a description of the problem so a plumber can be sent. We use that information to arrange and carry out the work and to follow up on it.

How we use information

We use what you give us to schedule service, give you a price, improve how we answer calls and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

We share the details needed for a job with the plumber who performs it. We may also share information when the law requires it.

Cookies and analytics

The site may use basic analytics to learn which pages are visited. You can limit cookies in your browser settings.

Your choices

To ask about the information we hold or to ask us to delete it, email contact@proplumbingdanapoint.com or call (949) 325-3122.

Changes

We may update this page. The date at the top of the site's pages reflects the current year, 2026.
