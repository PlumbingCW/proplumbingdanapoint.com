Urgent situations

When a failed sewer line becomes an emergency

A line that is leaking or partially collapsed may only cause slow drains for weeks. It becomes an emergency the day it fully blocks, or the day a leak undermines soil under a slab, driveway or hillside.

  • ✓
    Sewage backs up and a cable does not hold. If the line clears and fails again within days, the pipe itself is the problem.
  • ✓
    A sunken patch above the line. Soil washed into a broken pipe leaves a void that settles.
  • ✓
    Sewer smell with no visible backup. Gas escaping from a crack can reach the yard or crawlspace before any water appears.
  • ✓
    Water pooling in the yard with no rain or irrigation. Wet ground over the line can mean a leaking joint or a break.
Reading the camera

What the camera usually finds

A camera run shows the pipe from the inside and records distance, so the repair target is exact. Common findings are a single crack, an offset joint where two sections have shifted, a belly that holds water, root masses entering at joints and a complete collapse.

A localized crack with root intrusion is one of the most common repair requests. If the rest of the line is sound, a spot repair at that location is often the sensible choice, and the plumber should show you the footage so you can see why.

Repair options

Dig, line or replace?

Three approaches handle most failed sewer lines. The right one depends on the pipe, the length of damage and what is on top of the line.

Trenchless lining

  • A resin-coated liner is pulled into the pipe and cured in place
  • Minimal digging, which helps with driveways and landscaping
  • Works best on pipes that are still round and continuous
  • A camera confirms the pipe is a candidate

Open-trench spot repair or replacement

  • Replaces a broken section directly
  • Needed where a pipe has collapsed or sagged badly
  • More disruption to yard and surface
  • Often the more durable fix for a damaged section

A line that is blocked but not broken may only need sewer line cleaning or hydro jetting. A camera look is how you know which side you are on.

Local plumbing

Older homes, older pipe materials

Capistrano Beach started building out in 1925, and Lantern Village holds many of Dana Point's oldest houses. Sewer pipe from that period can be clay, cast iron or fiber. Each fails differently. Clay cracks at joints, cast iron rusts from the inside out and fiber can soften and deform.

If you are repairing a cast iron section, you will often hear about cast iron pipe repair, which usually means cutting out the corroded stretch and replacing it with modern pipe, or lining it where the pipe is otherwise sound. Ask the plumber which they recommend and why.

Septic tanks are uncommon in Dana Point because South Coast Water District provides wastewater service, but a property that predates the sewer or sits outside it can have one. Say so on the call, and the dispatcher will tell you whether it is a job we can send help for.

Before the dig

Questions to ask before approving any repair

Sewer repair is one of the larger plumbing bills a homeowner faces, and a second opinion is reasonable. These questions keep the conversation honest.

  • ✓
    What did the camera show? Ask to see the footage and the distance measurement.
  • ✓
    Is a spot repair enough? A single crack does not always need a whole line replacement.
  • ✓
    Does the work need a permit? Dana Point's Building and Safety Division issues plumbing permits and inspections. Ask the plumber whether the job needs one and who pulls it.
  • ✓
    What happens to the surface? Concrete, landscaping and irrigation lines on top of the repair are part of the plan, not an afterthought.
  • ✓
    What is the price before work starts? A clear price in writing is the minimum.
Pricing

What affects the price of sewer line repair

The range for sewer repair is wide because no two lines are alike. A short spot repair in an open yard costs far less than a long replacement under a driveway.

Length of damageA few feet of repair costs a fraction of a full run.
Depth and accessA shallow line in an open yard is cheaper than a deep one under concrete.
MethodLining and trenching have different cost structures and different lifespans.
Surface restorationLandscaping, hardscape and irrigation affect the total.
PermitsAsk the plumber whether the job needs one and who handles it.

Insurance may cover resulting damage but typically not the cost of repairing a worn pipe. Ask your insurer before the work begins.
