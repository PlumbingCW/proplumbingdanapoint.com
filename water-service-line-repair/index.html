Emergency Water Service Line Repair in Dana Point, CA

The water service line is the buried pipe that carries water from the meter to your house. When it leaks, pressure drops, the yard turns soggy and the meter keeps turning even with every fixture off.

Emergency water service line repair in Dana Point starts with a call to the 24/7 line. Close the home's main valve, check the meter and tell the dispatcher what it did. That one detail tells the plumber whether the leak is on this line.

Is it the service line?

Signs the buried water line is leaking

Service line leaks are easy to miss because the pipe is underground. The signs show up in the yard, the meter and the faucets.

  • ✓
    The meter moves with the main valve closed. If shutting the main inside the house does not stop the meter, the leak is between the meter and that valve.
  • ✓
    A wet or sunken patch in the yard. Water travels along the pipe and surfaces at a low point, sometimes far from the leak.
  • ✓
    Low pressure at every faucet. A large leak upstream of the house leaves less pressure for fixtures.
  • ✓
    A hissing sound near the meter. A pressurized leak near the meter or the house wall is often audible.
  • ✓
    A bill that climbs for no reason. Water lost underground still passes through the meter.
Low pressure

Low water pressure that is not the fixture

A single faucet with low flow usually has a clogged aerator or a partly closed valve. If every fixture is weak, the cause is upstream: a pressure regulator that has failed, a partly closed main valve, a corroded line or a leak in the service line.

Pressure that is too high is a problem too. High pressure strains fixtures, valves and appliances. A plumber can test the pressure at a hose bib with a gauge and tell you whether a regulator needs replacing.

Repair choices

Repair a section or replace the line?

Which to choose depends on the pipe's condition and how many leaks it has had.

Spot repair

  • Digs to the leak and replaces a short section
  • Lower cost and quicker
  • Fits an isolated failure on otherwise sound pipe
  • Further leaks may appear on an old line

Full replacement

  • Replaces the pipe from meter to house
  • Better when the pipe has leaked repeatedly
  • May use trenchless methods to reduce digging
  • Higher cost, longer life

If you are not sure where the leak is, leak detection confirms the location before any digging starts.

Local plumbing

Where the district's side ends in Dana Point

South Coast Water District supplies water to Dana Point. The district's responsibility generally ends at the meter, and the pipe from there to your house belongs to you, though the exact boundary can differ by property. Ask the district to confirm where it ends at your address before paying for work near the meter.

Digging is a bigger deal than it sounds. Before any trench is opened, utility lines must be located, and a permit may be needed. The Dana Point Building and Safety Division can say whether a service line replacement needs one. Call (949) 248-3564 and ask.

Hillside lots in Dana Point can have long service runs and changes in grade. Both affect pressure and the cost of trenching, which is part of why a clear plan and a clear price should come first.

Materials

What an older service line may be made of

Service lines installed decades ago used different materials from those used today, and each has its own failure mode.

Galvanized steel

Rusts from the inside, narrows, loses pressure and eventually leaks.

Copper

Long-lived, but can develop pinholes or fail at joints.

Polyethylene

Flexible and common for newer lines, though poor installation can cause kinks.

Mixed materials

Repairs over the years can leave dissimilar metals joined, which corrode at the joint.

Codes and sources

What code and which office apply

The service line between the meter and the house is regulated by the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and the water side of the meter belongs to the utility. South Coast Water District, which serves Dana Point, is the contact for anything on the street side.
